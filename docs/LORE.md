# Lore: the Sygmension

The story SkyCraft's worlds are built around. Approved story beats only; the details are still open.

## The Sygmension

Every world sits somewhere in the **Sygmension**. Worlds gather into families, and inside a family the
worlds that look alike sit close together: one step across the Sygmension lands you in a world almost the
same as the one you left. That resemblance rule decides where every crossing goes.

Two families matter here:

- **The Kalpa family**: Skyrim and the worlds like it. SkyCraft's mirror world (Minecraft laid over
  Skyrim, the overworld of the SkyCraft save) is a Kalpa world seen through Minecraft's eyes.
- **The Seed family**: Minecraft's worlds, one for every seed.

## Steve

Steve is a loner. He walked east from spawn for years, past the last hills of his seed and into the Far
Lands, where the land breaks into towering layered walls. He walked through a crack in the wall, fell
between the families, and landed in Skyrim at **Darkwater Crossing**.

## The Dwemer

The Dwemer vanished from the Kalpa family because they escaped it: they crossed into the Seed family. The
world they reached, one step from Skyrim, is **Tamriel Wilds**: Skyrim more or less, shaped by the Seed
family's rules. Their bronze towers still stand there.

## The Nether

In the Seed family the Nether is where the family borders **Oblivion** (the Deadlands). Ruined portals in
Tamriel Wilds' mountains are Oblivion's touch on the Seed side.

## The keys

A crack only opens onto a place that resembles the one it starts from, and only onto one: portals are
locked pairs. A soul gem binds two cracks inside Skyrim. To reach the Seed family a crack needs something
that already belongs to both sides:

- **A Daedra heart** opens a crack in Skyrim onto **the Nether**, where the Seed family borders Oblivion.
- **A tonal key** opens one onto **Tamriel Wilds**, the Dwemer's side: a key of dwarven metal, redstone and
  amethyst, made the way the Dwemer built (tonal architecture).

Each key opens a crack at the other world's arrival point and is spent there. The pair stays locked: the
crack leads back to the portal in Skyrim and nowhere else.

## The ending

The story ends back in **Home**, Steve's old seed, at the edge of the Far Lands beside his last camp. The
way there is the **Time-Wound** on the summit of the Throat of the World: a crack lit there opens onto Home
for whoever carries an **Elder Scroll**. It opens one way; the story ends there.

In the Far Lands' broken strata, and in the crack Steve walked through, lies **Seamstone**. Picking it up is
the ending ("Past the Far Lands"). There Steve gains the power of **sygmensional travel**: Seamstone breaks
the pairing lock, so any crack opens onto any place he has been, across worlds and families at will.

## In the game

| World | Dimension | Family | What it is | The way in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mirror world | `minecraft:overworld` | Kalpa | Skyrim's shape in Minecraft; where SkyCraft is played | Where you start; soul gems bind its cracks in pairs |
| Tamriel Wilds | `skycraft:tamriel_wilds` | Seed | Skyrim one step away: tall ranges, holds as biomes, Dwemer towers, Nordic barrows | A tonal key on a crack in Skyrim |
| The Nether | `minecraft:the_nether` | Seed | Vanilla Nether, the border with Oblivion | A Daedra heart on a crack in Skyrim |
| Home | `skycraft:home` | Seed | Steve's old seed: classic terrain ending in the Far Lands at 12,550,821, with his last camp and the crack | A crack at the Time-Wound, with an Elder Scroll; one way |
